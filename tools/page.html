Patch returns into returns

A pin sends the row's output into the column's input. Click a hole to set a pin, drag it up and down for level, double-click to pull it. The diagonal feeds a unit into itself — capped short of unity, so it rings rather than runs away. Echo into spring into room, room back into echo: a dub desk.

Matrix level · place · feed · sends — a row per part

Drum machine one box, a kit loaded into it, a strip on every lane

A kit is which voices the lanes are loaded with; the circuit is which analogue engine the analog kit is — two instruments, not two ends of a dial. TUNE and DECAY on a strip are offsets on what the circuit already decided, and on a recording they are a playback rate and where to stop. The sends are the machine's individual outputs: one lane into a return the rest of the kit does not feed.
