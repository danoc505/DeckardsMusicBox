## THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST — and it is a failure, not a feature

**Phase 1 shipped an instrument that made no sound, and every check was green.**

`harness/fiddle_bank.py` wrote *sample* offsets where every other encoder writes
*byte* offsets. Two samples to a byte, so every address doubled: **37 of 75 rows
addressed past the end of the payload and decoded to silence; 38 decoded the
wrong note; one was right by luck.**

The fiddle drew in 17 of 40 records. It took both the tune and the answer. It
obeyed a bowing discipline built from sourced stroke lengths — 4,407 events, 6
bow changes, 0 gaps. **And it was silent.**

The battery was green. `probe_rack` said the panel named a box that played,
312/312. `probe_reachable` said every configured name happens.
`probe_controls` said all five fiddle knobs moved the sound.

**Every one of those asks about notes. A note is not audio.**

It surfaced only because Phase 2 rendered the washboard and found it
contributing 0.00 dB to a kit it was playing 19 strokes into.

`harness/probe_bank.js` now closes that hole and is in the battery: every sample
in every bank decoded, checked for addressable / audible / not-pinned-garbage.
308 pass. **It was run against a known-broken copy and watched failing on 37** —
which is why it takes a path argument.

## WHAT EACH PHASE ACTUALLY MOVED

### 1 — the bow
72 Philharmonia violin notes, G3–G6, three dynamics; the dynamic picks the
*recording*, not a volume. **A fourth playing discipline**: a bow runs out of
arm, not air, so a long note is *split* and the halves touch — no gap, unlike a
wind. Two pre-existing crashes fixed (the battery had been *aborting*, not
failing, since the washtub landed).

### 2 — the scrape
Three real washboard scrapes replace an 18-noise-tick model. They differ in
**hand speed** (57/98/150 ticks a second), not loudness, so the take is chosen
by room and weight. It plays the **ride** lane — which fired **zero times in 30
records** before, so the genre also had to ask. Level set by rendering: the first
constant made the timekeeper louder than the whole kit.

### 3 — development
`verseHook` had made an 8-bar material **the same two bars four times** — 38 of
60 verses perfectly periodic. Now bars 0–3 restate exactly (so the ear learns it)
and 4–7 **develop**, by sequence, displacement or changed tail.

**Perfect looping 38/60 → 0/60, with only 7% more distinct material** — the
number that proves it varies rather than rewrites.

### 4 — the arrangement
`family` declared on 25 machines — the seam §7 said did not exist (*"no voice in
the program knows whether it is a wind or a brass"*). And Rimsky's re-entry rule,
which was **entirely unbuilt**: boxcar had **93% of re-entries in the "colourless"
middle, and every record began *and* ended mezzo**. Now 0%, 0/30, 0/30.

### 5 — section lengths
**Every section function had exactly one length, in every genre** — a boxcar
verse 16 bars 220 times out of 220. Now ~12% of eligible sections carry a
**cadential extension**, at the 1-in-7 proportion a repertoire survey gives. It
only ever *adds*, because cutting would stop the tune inside the development
phase 3 built.

## WHAT IS STILL WRONG — measured, not guessed

### THE BASS IS INAUDIBLE (task #126)

`probe_stems boxcarsynth 5 30 60`, on a seed where the bass plays 14 distinct
pitches:

| | |
|---|---|
| bass, rendered alone | **−53.9 dB** |
| lead, rendered alone | −16.1 dB |
| **MISSED(A) — how much quieter the mix gets without it** | **0.00 dB** |

**That is the same number the sustained pad-bass had — the one the washtub was
built to replace.** Raising the string's feedback (a 0.2 s bass note is a click,
so that change stands) moved it ~2 dB and left MISSED(A) at 0.00. The buses are
level (`LEVEL.bass` 0.26 vs `lead` 0.28), so the loss is inside the synthesis —
the same class the banjo and harmonica needed measured trims for.

Also found, **not caused by this session** (identical before and after): the
bass sits on **one pitch for the whole record in 7 of 30 boxcar records**.

### Eleven bank samples are off their declared root (task #134)
`probe_bank` reports, and deliberately does not assert, 11 rows — banjo,
harmonica, bard wind — whose decoded pitch is 156–1119 cents from the root the
row claims. Either the packs are mis-rooted or the estimator cannot read a reedy
tone. **The window was not widened to make it zero.**

### Seventy-two knobs no genre moves (task #123)
Mostly the flange, DP4, matrix, 303, dronebox and CS-80 rows left behind when
six genres were deleted. Three are the washtub's pan knobs, **deliberately**
unridden — the genre's own cited mixing law keeps the bass mono.

### Still unbuilt from score-craft §8
The crescendo by accumulation (spawn a doubling when the outer-voice span
widens), the two-slope crescendo, and short-vs-long build handling.

## THE METHOD LESSON, TWICE

**A measurement that finds a fault can score the fix as a failure.** The audit
asked *"is this material perfectly periodic?"* — correct of a wandering tune, and
satisfied by a loop. It cannot separate the two, so the loop shipped unreported.
Phase 3's guard asks the two halves separately.

**And a guard nobody has watched fail is a guard nobody knows works.** Every
probe added this session — `probe_bank`, `probe_develop`, `probe_arrange`,
`probe_lengths` — was run against a deliberately broken build and seen to fail
before being trusted.
