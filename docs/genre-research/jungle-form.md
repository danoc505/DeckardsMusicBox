## The architecture, with its own numbers

The template, per KAN (quotes): intro **32 bars** — *"drums and
atmosphere. Stripped-down, DJ-friendly"*, *"enough time for a DJ to mix
the previous track out and your track in cleanly"*; build **16 bars** —
*"tension rising into the first drop"*; **first drop 64 bars** — *"full
arrangement revealed"*, *"split into two 32-bar halves with variation"*;
mid-section 16-32; breakdown **32 bars** — *"emotional reset. Drums often
drop out entirely"*; second build 16; **second drop 64** — *"should hit
harder than the first"*, with *"additional layers or a different bass
patch"*; outro **32** — *"stripped-down mirror of the intro"*. Tracks run
*"5-7 minutes"*; EDMProd: *"uncommon to see DnB tracks under 4 minutes."*

This is a TWO-DROP architecture born of DJ culture (dubplates, blends) —
which is why a pop walk could never produce it and why jungle wearing
acid's table produced neither genre's truth.

## The plan (as landed in `GENRE.jungle.form.plan`)

In the program's vocabulary — drop = chorus (the full arrangement IS the
payoff), breakdown = bridge (jungle's bridge already sits the kit out):

    buildup:   verse/instrumental, 16-48 bars   — atmosphere, then tension
    drop:      chorus, 48-64 bars, ends on chorus — two 32-bar statements
    mid:       verse/instrumental, 16-48 bars
    breakdown: bridge, 16 bars                  — the drums leave
    build2:    verse/instrumental, 16-32 bars
    drop2:     chorus, 48-64 bars, ends on chorus — the peak, harder by
               construction: the last payoff carries chorusPeak energy and
               the arc's apex

With jungle's 16-bar intro/outro outside the plan: totals ~192-270 bars ≈
**4:22-6:40 at 160-176 bpm** — inside the sources' 4-7 minute band.

## Marked and refused

- "First bass hit at bar 17 of the intro" [KAN] — a role-entry nicety the
  arrangement layer could honor someday; NOT smuggled into the form plan.
- "Harder second drop via additional layers / different bass patch" — the
  peak section already carries more (chorusPeak energy, arc apex, variant
  lift); a dedicated second-drop material variant is a future materials
  idea, noted, not built.
- Budget steps [CHOSEN] within the sourced bar counts. The 1994-specific
  looseness (drumtrip: the era was dubplate-first, pre-formula) argues for
  the wide ranges kept here rather than the rigid template.
