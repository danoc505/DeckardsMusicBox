# Genre parameters — grounded in research (not guessed)

Per the project's research law ("web-search before designing any musical mechanism"),
the genre bounds are grounded in sources, not invented. Summary of what was verified and
corrected. **Key finding: jazz is NOT universal** — it belongs to the jazz-lineage genres,
not to the modal/folkloric ones.

| Genre | Tempo | Harmony / feel | Jazz lineage? | Source-affinity leans on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **lofi** | 70–90 (≈80–85) | boom-bap swing, ii-V-I with 7th/9th | **Yes** (jazz-hop) | bach, wjazz, session, grammar |
| **house** | 118–128 (124) | four-on-floor; *deep* house uses 7th/9th/11th | **Deep house yes** | wjazz, grammar |
| **jungle** | 160–176 | amen break, sub-bass; *intelligent* jungle is jazzy | **Intelligent/jazzstep yes** | wjazz, grammar |
| **citypop** | 95–115 | lush, moving, 7ths | **Yes** | bach, wjazz, grammar |
| **barber / wise** | 70–100 | hazy jazz-lofi | **Yes** | bach, wjazz, (folk), grammar |
| **dungeon** | **88–116** *(was 60–84)* | **modal drones, parallel/open 5ths, stepwise cadences, NO extended chords; MARCH drums** | **No** | **folk, session, grammar** (no jazz/bach) |
| **ambient** | 50–72 | drifting, no cadence | **No** | folk, session, grammar (no bach) |

## Corrections made this pass (all green, determinism intact)

- **dungeon tempo 60–84 → 88–116** — research puts dungeon synth at ~95–125 BPM; 60–84 was
  funereal-doom slow, not the processional "heroes down the halls" feel.
- **dungeon feel `sparse` → new `march`** — a processional spine (kick on the heavy downbeats
  0 & 8, backbeat snare 4 & 12 with martial ghost-rolls, no busy hats, no swing). Research:
  dungeon percussion is "martial snare/timpani, processional," not a driving kit.
- **dungeon melody density 0.25 → 0.20, leap 0.30 → 0.15** — droning, modal, stepwise (not leapy).
- **source affinities corrected** — dungeon & ambient are modal/folkloric, so their contour
  affinity drops Bach (functional-tonal, the opposite of modal drone) and jazz; jazz affinity
  now lives only on the jazz-lineage genres. house drops the stray folk affinity.
