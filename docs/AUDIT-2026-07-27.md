# Whole-program audit — 2026-07-27

Every region of `Improv Machine playable_BETA 0.1.html` was read line by line — synth,
effects chain, scheduler, slicer, sample runtime, all five engines, ghost, pipeline,
conductor, exports, UI wiring — and every suspected defect was verified by measurement
against the runnable engine (or the rendered audio) before being believed. 23 confirmed
defects were fixed in commit `89baccc`; the rest are listed under OPEN below, with line
references, so nothing found is lost.

## Why the work kept failing

The composition layer got genuinely better — the measurements that said so were real.
But between the notes and the speakers sit five layers, and **every one of them was
damaging the result, and nothing ever tested the output**:

1. **The validator ran 24× per song and was not idempotent.** The ghost's harmony duck
   compounded (0.45^n) until 40.5% of every comp note sat at the inaudible velocity
   floor. The comp — the glue — was being silenced by the thing meant to protect the
   song. No note-level test can see a velocity that is technically present.
2. **Performance passes replaced what upstream passes established.** The kit's micro
   remap z-scored SWING as if it were jitter: every downbeat 20–55 ms early, hits
   ±132 ms. A drum pattern can be perfectly correct on the grid and still not sound
   like a pattern.
3. **Gain staging and the palette.** The lead was the quietest voice in the program
   (no per-oscillator gain on multi-osc voices: choir 1.20, pad 0.80, lead 0.48);
   both "soft" saturators were brick-wall clippers; 44.5% of house kicks flat-topped.
4. **Live and export were different programs.** The exported WAV — the thing actually
   being judged — carried up to double the reverb of live playback (hardcoded
   0.30/0.75 vs the genre's space), no melodic-chop logic, no low-cut into the wash.
   And the live reverb STACKED a new instance on every press of play.
5. **The tests measured the wrong population and the wrong layer.** Everything ran on
   note data, at seeds 1–40, before the ghost re-runs and the performance layers.
   `harness/test_output.js` + `test_output.py` now render one song per genre through
   the real `scheduleAll` and assert on the waveform: audibility, clipping, DC, crest,
   dynamics, band balance, and event-level determinism.

The repeated pattern behind almost every finding: **a later pass could not see what an
earlier pass had established, and nothing measured the seam.** That is also the honest
diagnosis of the process failure: fixes were validated where they were made, not where
they are heard.

## OPEN — found, verified, not yet fixed

- `scheduleAll` lacks the live melodic-chop + make-room path (~60 lines): a sample-led
  song exports as a different arrangement. (schedule ~9391–9453 vs scheduleAll.)
- Comp re-placement drops surplus strikes (10.4%) and truncates durations; it also
  re-draws per song bar while the bass is not re-placed, weakening the hand lock it
  exists to serve.
- Wet returns (convolver, MI reverb, delay) bypass the compressor/saturator/analyser
  and arrive ~6 ms before the dry path (compressor lookahead): pre-echo on transients,
  and the clip lamp cannot see reverb clipping.
- `exportMidi`/`_collectMelody` omit tensionHalf, pre-drop emptying, leadLift and the
  dyn curve; exports contain notes playback never sounds. SWAM/MPE exports have
  note-off/channel-steal defects.
- `timeStretch` output is ~N too long and stops N+seek early: stretched loops gain
  trailing silence and lose their tail.
- `findDownbeat` scores only low-band energy (comment promises harmony too) and picked
  phase 2 on a downbeat-at-zero break; `alignZeroCrossing` mixes samples with
  amplitude×1000 in one cost.
- `scoreSong.sFull` still rewards fullness (best-of-8 selects ~3% busier than raw —
  measured small, but it leans against the space work); the audition also never sees
  `_sampleCycle`.
- `loopBars` is a constant 4 — every corpus progression is exactly 4 chords, so the
  `>4 → 8` branch is unreachable and PHRASE(8)/DEVLOOP(4) live on different grids.
- Conductor overrides (genre/key) skip RNG draws, so "tune band to sample" produces an
  unrelated song, and `__keyOverride`/`__chordOverride` are never cleared.
- The section sizer multiplies 4-bar intros to 12 bars (up to ~58 s of intro at
  ambient tempi); duration targets miss by ±40%.
- `keyShift` is hardcoded 0 — the documented bridge key change never happens.
- Cold-ending clamp is partially undone by groove/prominence/kit passes.
- `modal()` ignores dur/tone and rings 2.2 s regardless; snare/clap create a dangling
  gain node per hit; the wash+riser pair at a moment seam is unducked broadband noise.
- Fill/empty grammar: fills are feel-blind outside breaks (ambient gets rock tom
  fills); fills drop the hats for their bar; `SCHEME`'s B-slot is a no-op up to 31% of
  the time (substitute on a non-hat).
- Latent (cannot fire today, will fire on the next change): `layOut`/`peakI` index the
  pre-split section list; `Math.max(8, loopBars)` hardcodes the two-loop window;
  `DRUM_MAP[lane] || "hat"` masks unknown lanes as hi-hats; `T.QUALITY` lacks
  `min7`/`dim`; two byte-identical `closeStack` implementations; `SONG_LIBRARY` is
  read by nothing.
