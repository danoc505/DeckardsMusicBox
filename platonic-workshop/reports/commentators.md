# The Greek commentators on Aristotle: what the import found

Written by `tools/aristotle_commentators.py` when the library is built (Aristotle milestone A5); every figure is counted from the build.

The text is First1KGreek's TEI of the *Commentaria in Aristotelem Graeca* (CC BY-SA 4.0), read by the Berlin pages. A page is linked to a column of Aristotle when the editor prints the column's reference in the margin (*printed*), or when the page repeats at least two runs of five words found in that column and in no other column of the work (*quoted*, ≈: a lemma, or a quotation from elsewhere in the work).

**6,519 pages linked: 2,029 links printed, 6,382 quoted.** 19 printed references name no column of the Workshop's text and are left unlinked.

**Not here, and why.**

- Philoponus, Against Proclus on the Eternity of the World, and the other CAG volumes not in First1KGreek (Themistius' paraphrases, Dexippus, Michael of Ephesus on the Ethics and the Parva naturalia, Eustratius, Stephanus): not in First1KGreek at the pinned commit; they are on the Internet Archive as scans and can be added as OCR, as the Neoplatonists of milestone 25.
