# The Works of Plato, translated by Sydenham and Taylor (1804): OCR text

**What this is.** Plain-text OCR of *The Works of Plato, viz. his fifty-five
dialogues and twelve epistles, translated from the Greek; nine of the dialogues
by the late Floyer Sydenham, and the remainder by Thomas Taylor*, 5 vols,
London, 1804. The scans are of the AMS Press (New York, 1972) facsimile. The
files are Internet Archive "djvu.txt" exports.

**Quality.** Unproofread. The 1804 printing uses the long s (ſ), which the OCR
reads as "f" ("fuch", "thefe", "univerfe"): about 9% of words in the Timaeus
translation (see `reports/verification.md`). Running headers and page numbers
are mixed into the text, footnotes are interleaved with it, words are
hyphenated across lines, and Greek is garbled. No Stephanus numbers are printed.

**Attribution.** Nine dialogues are Sydenham's, revised by Taylor; the rest are
Taylor's. Each dialogue will be attributed to its actual translator when it is
imported (milestone 6), not to Taylor wholesale.

These files are kept exactly as received. Cleanup writes new files elsewhere.
