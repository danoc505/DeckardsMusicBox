Tap any Greek word for its dictionary form and grammar, explained in plain English from Smyth's grammar, and its entries in the two Greek-English dictionaries (Middle Liddell and LSJ).

Select Greek words (drag across them; on the iPad, press and hold the first) to write your translation of them, a footnote, a note or an alternative rendering.

Underlined words are key terms (the Good, intellect, soul, the One…): tap one for what it means and every place it is used, in the Term tab. Key terms above the text turns the underlines off.

Under each section, every commentator on it. Point at a note to light up the Greek words it is on (dotted in the text); click a dotted word to see the notes on it.

The numbers in the margin (29e) are Stephanus numbers, used by every book on Plato; tap one to copy it.

Open another dialogue from the menu at the top left, or type a work and a reference in the box (Rep. 514a). The ? Help button at the top says what each page is for; Words used in the Workshop explains the names.

Keys: ← → pages, g go to reference, f search everything, / concordance, Esc close.
